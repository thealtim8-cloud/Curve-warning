Welcome to Curve Guide

Curve Guide is a product of the Automotive Division of Adeniyi Investments & Property Ltd., built to help prevent road accidents on dangerous bends and curves. By giving drivers early awareness of sharp turns ahead, it supports safer, more confident driving on Nigerian roads. Curve Guide is currently a prototype: it is designed to supplement, never replace, careful and attentive driving.

“Prevention is better than cure.”

  1. Experimental prototype. Curve Guide is under development and is provided "as is", without warranties of any kind.
  2. Not a certified safety system. It is not an approved driver-assistance or navigation device, and its warnings may be missing, late, or wrong.
  3. You remain responsible. Always obey traffic laws, road signs, speed limits, and actual road conditions.
  4. Do not operate while driving. Set it up before your trip, or have a passenger operate it.
  5. Location privacy. Your exact GPS location is processed only on your device, in your browser. Curve Guide does not store it. When you search, your search text and a rough area (about 10 km) are sent to free OpenStreetMap search services to find nearby places.
